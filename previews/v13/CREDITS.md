# The Last Observatory — V13 credits

Original scene design, editing, procedural Astra galaxy, adapted character performance, archery correction and film assembly: this project. The V13 picture is rendered at native 1920×1080.

## Character and motion

Microsoft Rocketbox **FemaleAdult04**, including its native locomotion library: Microsoft Rocketbox, MIT license. Adapted materials, facial acting, gaze, bow-motion retargeting, arm targeting and fingertip gesture are project modifications. New run assets and their pinned source are recorded in `assets/character/heroine_v12/acquisition.json`.

Bow choreography and props: Kevin Iglesias, **Human Archer Animations FREE**, supplied by the user under the Standard Asset Store EULA. Roll source: Quaternius Universal Animation Library. The original source packs and Blender scenes containing reusable licensed data stay local.

Bear: [Realistic Animated Bear 3D Model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/realistic-animated-bear-3d-model-bffc3c87d2d148ff8533e1cc8a11c9f1), uploaded by **WildMesh 3D**, declared [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) by its uploader. Project modifications include placement, animation blending, jaw limitation, floor correction and arrow impact attachment. See the local source manifest for the supplied archive's provenance.

## Environment and sky

Poly Haven and ambientCG: CC0 scanned surfaces, fir trees, saplings, ferns, grasses, mossy rocks, cliff and forest floor. Reused sci-fi parts: Irondust and rubberduck/a52, CC0. Observatory architecture and mechanism include custom project geometry.

Star map: [NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851/), with Gaia DR2 data credited to ESA/Gaia/DPAC. The central Astra galaxy is procedural project artwork.

## Music

**Beyond the Threshold** — original composition, arrangement and production for this film. Instrument recordings: **VSCO 2 Community Edition**, Versilian Studios, CC0-1.0. Library credits include Sam Gossner, Simon Dalzell and Elan Hickler/Soundemote.

Music source files, exact recording credits, cue timings and mastering measurements are documented in `audio/v13/README.md` and `audio/v13/sources/acquisition.json`. The composition is new project work; the CC0 statement applies to the source instrument recordings.
